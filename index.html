What PatientStars Does

Two things, done well.

PatientStars connects directly to your Google Business Profile via OAuth. When a patient leaves a review, the AI writes a response — warm, professional, and scanned to make sure nothing in it could run afoul of HIPAA. You get a notification, read the draft, and post it with one click. That's it.

The second piece is local visibility tracking. PatientStars shows you how your practice ranks on Google Maps across a grid of your surrounding area, updated on a regular schedule, so you actually know whether your profile is performing — not just what your star rating is.

Why This Matters

Three ways an inactive
Google profile costs you.

01

Lower position in the local map pack

Google uses review engagement as a local ranking factor. Practices that respond to reviews consistently tend to show up higher when someone nearby searches for a dentist.

02

Prospective patients move on

When someone researching a new dental practice sees an unanswered review — especially a negative one — the silence reads as disinterest. They click to the next result.

03

Manual responses create HIPAA risk

Replying to reviews by hand — especially to upset patients — can accidentally surface protected health information. PatientStars scans every draft before it's ever visible to you.
